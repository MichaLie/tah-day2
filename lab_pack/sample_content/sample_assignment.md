## Module: Introduction to Digital Society (first-year, interdisciplinary)

### Assignment: Critical essay
**Title:** "Choose one digital technology that has changed higher education in the last decade. Argue whether its overall effect has been positive or negative, using evidence."

**Length:** 1,500 words.
**Weight:** 30% of module mark.

**Learning outcomes assessed:**
1. Construct a clear, evidence-based argument.
2. Locate and correctly cite relevant academic and reputable sources.
3. Evaluate competing perspectives on a contested issue.
4. Write in clear, well-structured academic prose.

**Requirements:**
- At least five sources, three of them peer-reviewed.
- Harvard referencing.
- Submit as PDF via the VLE.

**Marking criteria (summary):** argument & structure (30%), use of evidence (30%), critical evaluation (25%), writing & referencing (15%).
