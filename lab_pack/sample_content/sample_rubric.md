# Sample Rubric (green) — Critical Essay
*Pairs with sample_assignment.md. Use in Track 3 for rubric-based feedback drafting.*

| Criterion | Excellent | Good | Developing | Insufficient |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Argument & structure** (30%) | Clear thesis sustained throughout; logical progression; each paragraph advances the argument | Clear thesis; mostly logical; occasional digression | Thesis present but wavers; structure uneven | No clear thesis; disorganized |
| **Use of evidence** (30%) | ≥5 relevant sources, ≥3 peer-reviewed, accurately interpreted and integrated | Adequate sources, mostly well used; minor misreadings | Few or weak sources; evidence loosely connected | Little or no credible evidence; sources misrepresented |
| **Critical evaluation** (25%) | Weighs competing perspectives; acknowledges complexity and counter-arguments | Considers more than one view; some evaluation | Mostly descriptive; limited evaluation | One-sided; no engagement with alternatives |
| **Writing & referencing** (15%) | Fluent, precise; Harvard referencing correct throughout | Clear; minor referencing slips | Frequent lapses in clarity or referencing | Hard to follow; referencing largely absent or wrong |

*Note: weightings shown are the module leader's; adapt for your own context.*
