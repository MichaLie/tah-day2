# Student E

digital tech has changed uni a lot in the last ten years. the biggest one is probably the phone and apps because everyone has one now.

with apps you can check your timetable and get notifications and use the library app to find books. this is good because its convenient and saves time. also you can use quizlet and other apps to revise which helps memory.

but there are bad things too. phones are distracting in lectures and people go on instagram instead of listening. studies show that screen time is bad for attention. so its a distraction.

another point is that not everyone can afford the latest phone so there is a digital divide which is unfair.

in conclusion phones and apps are good and bad but i think mostly good because they are convenient and thats what students want in the modern world. technology is the future so we have to accept it.
