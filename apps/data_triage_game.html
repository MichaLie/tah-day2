Data Triage — Green · Amber · Red

Before any task goes near an AI tool, classify the data it touches. 12 situations from academic life — what's your call?

🟢 public or synthetic → go  ·  🟠 internal, needs permission & care → think first  ·  🔴 personal / confidential / protected → never in consumer tools

Keyboard: press 1 = green, 2 = amber, 3 = red

The rules of thumb worth keeping:
· Peer-review manuscripts are red, not amber — publishers prohibit uploading them, and the NIH bans AI in review.
· Student writing needs an approved tool and informed students — anonymization alone is not a licence.
· "Temporary chat" and "training off" reduce exposure; they do not reclassify red data.
· Agents amplify everything: least privilege, copied folders, dummy data, no secrets.
· Sometimes the whole tool is the red line, whatever the data class.
